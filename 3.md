# Multitool 1.2.0

Fixed truncation, precisely, two call sites each drew at most one screen-width of text per logical line and silently discarded the rest
What was the fix?
a wrap_text() helper that inserts line breaks at the available width (preserving existing newlines), applied in both the feed and the dialog. 
The dialog's scroll/line-count also works on wrapped lines now. Verified: the ending of the yapm post (2.md, 2nd post) now appears fully in both views.
